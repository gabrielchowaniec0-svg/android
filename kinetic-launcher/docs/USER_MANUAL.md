# Kinetic: User Manual

Kinetic is your home screen for the Galaxy S25 Ultra. It has an iPhone-style
Dynamic Island, a living wallpaper, an encrypted Vault, S Pen commands and a
plane spotter. This manual explains how to use all of it, grouped by topic.

In this manual, **Settings** means *Kinetic settings*. To open it, go to the app
list, tap **⋮**, then **Kinetic settings**. You can also tap **Settings** on the
edit-mode bar. The search box at the top of Settings finds any switch: type
"volume" or "moon", for example.

## 1. Getting started

The **Setup** section at the top of Settings shows what's on and what still
needs switching on. Each item has a shortcut to the right page.

| What | Why | Where to switch it on |
|---|---|---|
| Default Home app | Makes Kinetic your home screen | Samsung Settings ▸ Apps ▸ Choose default apps ▸ Home app ▸ Kinetic |
| Notification access | Notification rings, badges, music and calls in the island | Settings ▸ Setup |
| Dynamic Island (Accessibility) | The island, status bar, home bar, volume slider, secure screenshot, double-tap to lock | Settings ▸ Setup, then switch on **Kinetic Dynamic Island** |
| Usage access (optional) | Commands use the app you use most for each job (Maps or Waze, and so on) | Settings ▸ Setup |
| Do Not Disturb access | Focus silencing, "snooze notifications", "silent" | Asked the first time you need it |

If the Dynamic Island switch is greyed out, go to Samsung Settings ▸ Apps ▸
Kinetic ▸ **⋮** ▸ **Allow restricted settings** first.

The Dynamic Island switch is an accessibility service because Android only lets
those draw above the status bar. Kinetic doesn't read what's on your screen. It
only notices *which* app is open, for example so that a song stays hidden while
you're in the app playing it.

**Optional Good Lock setup** (to replace Samsung's bars with Kinetic's):
- Good Lock ▸ **QuickStar**: turn off every indicator icon and hide the clock.
  Then in Samsung Settings ▸ Notifications ▸ Advanced settings, set **Show
  notification icons** to None. Then switch on Settings ▸ Status bar, home bar &
  buttons ▸ **Kinetic status bar**.
- Good Lock ▸ **NavStar**: hide the gesture hint. Then switch on **Kinetic home
  bar** in the same Settings section.

## 2. Home screen and app list

**Moving around**
- Swipe left or right to change home pages.
- Swipe up anywhere to open the app list. It follows your finger. Swipe down to
  close it.
- Swipe down anywhere for the notification shade. Swipe down from the top-right
  corner for Android's Quick Settings.

**Opening apps**
- Apps zoom open out of their icon. When you come home, the icon gives a small
  landing bounce. (Android doesn't let launchers shrink an app back into its
  icon.)
- Long-press an app for a pop-up with the app's own shortcuts (for example "New
  message"), plus App info, Edit home, Remove or Add to home, More… and
  **Uninstall** (only for apps you installed yourself).

**Editing the home screen**
- Icons stay put until you edit. Press and hold empty wallpaper for about a
  second to start editing.
- Drag icons, folders and widgets to move them. They snap to half-cells. If you
  drag onto another icon's spot, that icon slides out of the way.
- **Make a folder:** hold an app over the middle of another app for about half a
  second. The target swells; let go.
- **Put an app into a folder:** hold the app, keep holding, and drag it onto the
  folder. This works on the home screen and in the app list.
- Tap a folder in edit mode for **Make large (2 × 2)** or **Make small**.
- Widgets have a round stretch handle at the bottom right. Drag it to resize. It
  says "Not enough room" if something's in the way.
- The bottom bar has **Add apps**, **Widgets**, **Wallpaper**, **+ Page**,
  **Delete page** (empty pages only), **Settings** and **Done**. Back also
  finishes editing.
- The first time you add a widget, Android asks you to allow Kinetic to add
  widgets. Tap **Allow**, and choose "always" if offered.

**Widget stacks**
- In edit mode, tap a widget ▸ **Add widget to stack…**. The two now share one
  spot. Swipe up or down on it to flip between them.
- **Smart rotate** (on by default) shows what fits: the music app's widget while
  music plays, calendar or weather in the morning, notes in the day, weather or
  battery in the evening. If you flip it yourself, it waits 3 minutes before
  rotating again.

**Your layout**
- Your S25 Ultra layout is built in: widgets, the 2 × 2 Google folder,
  Chowaniec, the dock, and your app-list pages and folders.
- To start over: app list ▸ **⋮** ▸ **Reset to my phone's layout**.

**App list extras** (app list ▸ ⋮)
- Show hidden apps (asks for your Kinetic code), **Sort A–Z**, **App Library
  view** (apps grouped into Social, Games and so on), Vault, Focus, Plane
  spotter, Commands.
- Apps you install wait in a **New** folder for a week.

**Look**
- **Notification rings:** a thin spinning ring around apps with notifications.
  **Number badges** show the unread count. Both are in Settings ▸ Home screen.
- **Icon style** (Settings ▸ Icons & pages): Normal, Tinted, Glass or Mono.
- **Page transition:** Depth, Flat, Cube, Carousel or Fade.
- Tilting the phone makes the home screen lean slightly, and the wallpaper
  layers shift (parallax).

**Chowaniec**
- Long-press Chowaniec for Group Chat, Messages, Chowaniec AI, Notes and
  Settings.
- In search, type a page name (`notes`, `calendar`, `games`…) to jump straight
  to it. You can also use **Search Chowaniec for …**.

**Search** (the pill at the bottom of the app list)
- Finds apps (typos are fine: "spotfy" finds Spotify), contacts, Settings pages,
  quick maths (`15% of 80`, `2^10`), Chowaniec pages, and anything in the
  [command list](#6-command-list).
- Hold the Search pill to say it instead of typing.

## 3. The Dynamic Island

A black, iPhone-style pill around the front camera, above every app.

**Touching it**
- **Tap:** opens the app behind what it's showing.
- **Hold, or pull down:** opens it bigger. It follows your finger. Let go past
  about a third of the way (or flick) and it finishes opening; otherwise it
  settles back.
- **Push up** on a big island to fold it.
- **Swipe sideways:** when two or more things are live, brings the next one to
  the front. Dots under the island show which one you're on.
- **Swipe up on a message or question** to dismiss it.
- **Tap outside** to fold it. It also folds by itself after a few seconds, and
  when the screen turns off.
- While resting, it lets touches through, so pulling the shade down from the
  middle still works. It hides when the phone is sideways (most full-screen
  videos and games). While it's hidden, the volume buttons show Samsung's own
  volume panel, and Kinetic's messages appear as short pop-ups at the bottom.

**What it shows**
- **Music and video:** album art and a waveform tinted from the art. Opened:
  title, progress bar (tap to seek), previous, play/pause, next. A song doesn't
  show while you're in the app playing it. When it's paused and you're elsewhere,
  it shrinks to the app's logo after a short while.
- **Calls:** a green phone with a timer. Incoming calls open with Answer and
  Decline.
- **Timers and alarms** from the Clock app. Alarms ring in the island with
  Snooze and Stop.
- **Directions** (Google Maps, Waze): the next turn and distance.
- **Deliveries and rides** (Uber, DoorDash, Lyft and others): ETA and progress.
- **Live scores** (ESPN, theScore and others). Settings ▸ What shows in the
  island ▸ **My teams** limits it to your teams, and the island bounces when they
  score.
- **Progress bars:** downloads and installs, with a tick when done.
- **Messages:** Chowaniec messages drop down with the sender. Tap **Reply** to
  answer right there. Other apps can do the same (Settings ▸ What shows in the
  island ▸ *Messages from all apps*).
- **Kinetic's own live activities:** live stopwatch, Focus ring, Up next (15
  minutes before a calendar event), plane watch, tracked flights.
- **Flashlight** (see below).
- **Pop-ups:** charging (with "Full at …"), earbuds connecting (with battery),
  silent/vibrate/ring, low battery at 15% (tap for Battery Saver), rain starting
  soon, Focus on/off, and confetti when you finish something.
- **Privacy dots:** orange when an app uses the mic, green for the camera, red
  while the screen is recorded.
- **Two things at once:** the second one shows as a small bubble beside the pill.

**Flashlight, like iOS 18**
- When the flashlight is on (however you turned it on), the island shows a small
  flashlight icon.
- Tap it to open a flashlight shining its beam upward. Drag up for brighter (the
  beam reaches further) and down for dimmer. It ticks at each step. Tap the
  flashlight to switch it off or on.
- While the flashlight is on, swiping up or down on the island controls the
  light instead of opening or closing the island.

**Volume and brightness sliders**
- The volume buttons show Kinetic's slider in the island. Calls still use
  Samsung's. Hold a button to keep changing the volume.
- **Volume buttons change:** Media or Ringtone (Settings ▸ Status bar, home bar
  & buttons). On Media, the ringtone is never changed by accident. While the
  phone is on silent or vibrate, the buttons always change media.
- A brightness slider appears when brightness changes, but only while
  auto-brightness is off. (Otherwise it would pop up all day.)

**Screenshot preview**
- After a normal screenshot, a preview drops out of the island for about 6
  seconds, with **Edit**, **Share** and **Vault**. *Vault* seals it into the
  Vault, then Android asks once to delete it from the Gallery.
- Switch it on in Settings ▸ What shows in the island ▸ **Screenshot preview**.
  Tap it to allow photo access.
- To show only Kinetic's preview, turn off Samsung's toolbar: Samsung Settings ▸
  Advanced features ▸ Screenshots and screen recordings ▸ **Show toolbar after
  capturing**.

**Unlock animation**
- When the screen wakes on the lock screen with the phone held up, the island
  shows a small Face ID–style face. It becomes a tick when face or fingerprint
  unlock works. If you stay on the lock screen, a padlock opens. If unlocking
  hasn't worked after about 3 seconds, the face shakes and fades.
- It doesn't show when the phone is flat on a table or in a pocket.

**Look** (Settings ▸ Dynamic Island)
- **Island look:** Black, Glass or Outline.
- **Music glow:** a soft glow in the album's colours while music plays.
- **Gap above the island**, **Show resting pill**, **Lock animation**.

## 4. Status bar and home bar

Both need the Dynamic Island switch on and the Good Lock setup from
[Getting started](#1-getting-started).

**Kinetic status bar**
- Left: the time and up to 3 notification icons, with a dot if there are more.
  On the lock screen it shows your carrier instead of the time.
- Right: Do Not Disturb, silent/vibrate, signal, Wi-Fi or 5G/LTE, and the
  battery. The battery is white with its number cut out. It turns red at 5% or
  less.
- When the island grows, each side keeps what fits, in order of importance
  (time and battery always stay), centred in the space that's left.
- **Status bar text:** Auto picks black or white for the app behind it. You can
  force White or Black.
- Full-screen videos and games hide it.

**Kinetic home bar**
- A bar at the bottom. It glows in the album's colours with music, pulses green
  on a call, takes your Focus colour, and sweeps green when you plug in.
  Otherwise it's plain white (black over light apps).
- It never takes touches, so swiping up for home and recents works as normal.

## 5. Writing and commands

You can give Kinetic commands in three ways:
- **Write with the S Pen** anywhere on the home screen. Pause and Kinetic reads
  it. Fingers work as normal; only pen strokes become ink. Handwriting is read
  on the phone (the first time, it downloads a 20 MB handwriting model).
- **Type in search** (the app list's Search pill). A card shows what will
  happen; tap the button or press Enter.
- **Say it:** hold the Search pill.

With the island on, written commands happen in the island:
- **Texts and calls** drop down with Send/Call and Cancel.
- **Answers** drop down for a few seconds.
- **Missing permissions** drop down with Allow and Not now, then carry on by
  themselves.
- **Anything it doesn't understand** offers Search and Web.

**Making Kinetic yours** (Settings ▸ S Pen)
- **Writing shortcuts:** `shortcut m = call mom`. Then writing "m" calls Mom.
  `remove shortcut m` deletes it.
- **Command chains:** one name runs several commands. For example, "gaming
  mode" = `focus 60 min`, then `open roblox`.
- **Shapes:** give a heart, circle, check, star or triangle a command. Draw it
  in one stroke to run it.
- `again` repeats your last command. `undo` puts back the last volume,
  brightness, ringer or Do Not Disturb change.
- Start a command you've used before ("ca") and the island offers to finish it.

**The full list on your phone:** write `help` or `?`, or open **Commands** in
the app list, or Settings ▸ S Pen ▸ **All commands**. Every command is grouped
and searchable, with a **Try** button. Your shortcuts, chains and recent
commands are at the top.

## 7. Focus

Open it from the app list ▸ ⋮ ▸ **Focus…**, Settings ▸ Personalise ▸ **Focus**,
or the **Focus** quick-settings tile. To add the tile, pull down the Quick
Panel, tap the pencil and drag *Focus* in. Tapping the tile turns your last
Focus on or off; long-pressing it opens the list.

- Built in: Do Not Disturb, Sleep, School, Gaming, Personal and Driving.
- Hold a Focus to change it: silence, let every app through, wallpaper dimming,
  which home page to jump to, and which apps may still show rings and island
  pop-ups.
- **Schedule:** pick start and end times and days. Overnight schedules (10:30 PM
  to 7:00 AM) work.
- **Place:** *Use my current location*, then 100, 200 or 500 m. It turns on
  when you arrive and off when you leave. This needs location "Allow all the
  time".
- **Apps:** turn on when you open certain apps (for example games for Gaming),
  and off when you leave them.
- **Driving** can turn on when your car's Bluetooth connects.
- **Focus timer:** write `focus 25 min`. Your Study/School Focus turns on, a
  ring empties in the island (pull it down for **End now**), and it turns off
  with a chime and confetti when the time's up.
- The island shows the Focus name when one switches, and keeps its icon next to
  the camera while it's on.

## 8. Wallpaper

**Setting it**
- Edit mode ▸ **Wallpaper** ▸ **Set wallpaper**. If One UI asks, choose **Home
  and lock screens**.
- If One UI only offers the home screen, Samsung is keeping the lock screen for
  its own wallpapers on that software version. Apps can't change that.

**Wallpaper studio** (edit mode ▸ Wallpaper, or Settings ▸ Personalise)
- **Styles:** Ribbons, Waves, Silk, Aurora, Orbs, Crystal, Dunes, Static. Each
  has its own ripple.
- **Colours:** 8 palettes, or build your own.
- **Feel:** speed, brightness, grain and detail. Changes apply live; tap the
  preview to see a ripple.

**Reactions** (Settings ▸ Wallpaper reactions; each can be turned off)
- A ripple when you unlock, from any app icon you open, a soft pulse for
  notifications, a green wave when you plug in, album colours while music plays.
- Home icons hop as a ripple passes them.
- Below 20% battery (or in Battery Saver) it slows down and dims.
- When you unlock straight to the home screen, the icons glide in along the
  wallpaper's flow lines.

**Living wallpaper** (Settings ▸ Living wallpaper)
- **Rain when it's raining**, and **Snow, fog & wind**, from the real weather
  where you are (rough location, checked every 30 minutes).
- **Battery tide:** a glowing waterline. Green while charging, red when low.
- **Fireflies:** one for each app with unread notifications.

**The living sky** (also Settings ▸ Living wallpaper)
- **Show the sky on:** Lock screen (default), Both, Home or Off. It fades in
  when you lock and out when you unlock.
- **Sunset & moon:** warmer colours at your real sunrise and sunset. At night,
  the real moon in tonight's phase, with craters and seas. Behind clouds it
  becomes a soft patch of moonlight.
- **Stars & planets:** about 340 stars and Venus, Mars, Jupiter and Saturn,
  where they really are, facing south. They turn slowly through the night.
  Optional **Constellation lines** for Orion, the Big Dipper and Cassiopeia.
- **Clouds** from the real cloud cover.
- **Planes & space station:** real planes within about 25 km, where they really
  are in your sky. At night they show a red light on the left wing, green on the
  right, white strobes and a red beacon. By day, a speck with a contrail. The
  space station shows as a steady bright point when it passes at night.
- Planes and the station are only looked up while the sky is on screen.

## 9. Privacy: Vault, code, secure screenshots and video

**The Vault** (app list ▸ ⋮ ▸ Vault, or Settings ▸ Privacy ▸ Vault)
- Keeps photos and videos encrypted inside Kinetic.
- **Add from gallery:** pick photos and videos; they're encrypted, then Android
  asks you to confirm deleting the originals. If storage is full, anything that
  couldn't be moved stays in your gallery and Kinetic tells you.
- **Camera** and **Video** take a photo or video straight into the Vault. It
  never appears in your gallery.
- Open an item ▸ **Move to gallery** to take it back out (to Pictures/Kinetic or
  Movies/Kinetic).
- Screenshots and the Recents preview are blocked, and the Vault locks the
  moment you leave it.
- Each file is encrypted with AES-256. The key is locked twice: with your code,
  and with a key in the phone's secure hardware. A copied file is useless on
  another phone or without your code.
- **No recovery.** If you forget the code, or uninstall Kinetic or clear its
  data, the Vault's contents are gone for good. Keep a backup of anything
  important elsewhere.

**Kinetic code**
- A 6-digit PIN or a pattern, chosen the first time. It protects the Vault,
  hidden apps and locked apps. It's separate from your phone's lock.
- Change it in Settings ▸ Privacy ▸ **Change Kinetic code**.
- After 5 wrong tries you wait 30 seconds, doubling each time (up to 15
  minutes).
- Why not one fingerprint? Android only tells apps "a fingerprint on this phone
  matched", never which finger, and apps can't add their own fingerprints.

**More protection** (Settings ▸ Privacy)
- **Decoy code:** a second code that opens a separate, fake Vault. It never
  shows hidden apps or opens locked apps.
- **Intruder photo:** after 3 wrong codes in a row, the front camera quietly
  takes one photo into the real Vault. On by default.
- **Lock apps:** long-press an app ▸ **Require Kinetic code**. Opening it from
  Kinetic then asks for your code. (Opening it from Recents or another app's
  link doesn't; Android doesn't let launchers block those.)

**Secure screenshot**
- Press **Volume Up, Up, Down, Down** quickly. The island's padlock closes and
  the phone buzzes. The screenshot goes straight into the Vault without asking
  for your code, and never appears in the gallery.
- It needs the Dynamic Island switch on and the Vault opened once. Turn it off
  in Settings ▸ Privacy ▸ **Secure screenshot**.
- The volume ends where it started. It doesn't run during calls or with the
  screen off. Apps that block screenshots (banking apps) come out black.
- How it's safe without a code: the screenshot is locked with a key that can
  only lock, not unlock. The key that opens it is kept inside the Vault.

**Secure video**
- Write `secure video` (or "private video"). A camera opens, also over the lock
  screen. Tap the red button to record and again to stop. ⟲ flips cameras.
- No code is needed to record. You need your code to watch it. It's in the Vault
  the next time you open it.
- Leaving the screen (home button, screen off) stops and saves the recording,
  and saving finishes even if you leave straight away.
- It needs about 300 MB free to start, and stops itself before the phone fills
  up, so there's always room to seal it.
- The Vault needs to have been opened once first.

## 10. Planes

**Plane spotter** (app list ▸ ⋮ ▸ Plane spotter, or write `planes`)
- Point the camera at a plane. When it lines up with the circle, it locks on
  and shows the flight number, airline, route, altitude, speed, distance and
  aircraft type. If nothing's in view, it tells you which way to turn.
- **Logbook:** every plane you lock onto is saved (once per flight per day),
  with totals and your most-spotted aircraft types.
- **United:** shows only United flights (mainline and United Express).
- **Radar:** a night view without the camera, with rings at 10, 20 and 35 miles.
  It also turns on if the camera isn't allowed.
- If labels drift sideways, calibrate the compass by waving the phone in a
  figure 8. Some small private planes don't share a route.
- Data comes from adsb.lol / adsb.fi, with routes from adsbdb.com.

**In the island**
- `track UA123` follows a flight live (climbing, cruising, landing soon) and
  celebrates when it lands.
- `plane watch` shows planes passing within about 4 miles for the next hour.

## 11. StandBy and tools

**StandBy** (Settings ▸ Home screen ▸ StandBy)
- Put the phone on the charger, turn it on its side and leave it still for a
  couple of seconds. You get a big clock, the date, your next event and the
  battery.
- Swipe sideways for photos or the calendar. Late at night or in the dark, it
  all turns deep red.
- To leave: unplug, stand the phone upright or swipe up.

**Tools:** `level`, `ruler` and `decibels` open a tools screen.

## 12. Gestures

Settings ▸ Gestures:
- **Double-tap empty wallpaper** to lock the phone (needs the Dynamic Island
  switch).
- **Two fingers down** opens Focus. **A three-finger tap** opens search and
  Commands.
- **Shake to…:** flashlight, camera, search, Focus or any app. Two firm shakes
  trigger it. Only the flashlight works while the phone is locked.

**Haptics:** the switch in Settings ▸ Home screen covers every buzz in Kinetic.

## 13. Battery, updates and problem reports

**Battery** (Settings ▸ Battery & help)
- **Wallpaper smoothness:** *Balanced* draws at 30 fps while nothing moves, and
  at your screen's full rate when you touch it or it reacts. *Always smooth*
  keeps the full rate. It follows your refresh-rate and resolution settings.
- In power saving mode, the wallpaper runs at 30 fps and the plane, space
  station and status-bar colour checks pause.

**Updates**
- Every merged change becomes an update you can install from Settings ▸
  **Updates** (one-time setup in UPDATES.md). Kinetic checks there's enough
  storage first and says if you're offline.
- Speed tip if you install from a computer: use the **release** build. Debug
  builds are noticeably slower.

**Report a problem**
- Write `report problem`, or Settings ▸ Battery & help ▸ **Report a problem**.
- Kinetic makes one text file: phone model and versions, what's switched on
  and allowed, Kinetic's settings (not your location, notes, homework or Vault),
  and Kinetic's own crash and error log.
- The share sheet opens so you can send it. Nothing is ever sent by itself.

## 14. When things go wrong

**After a restart or update**
- A running stopwatch (with laps), Focus session, notification snooze, plane
  watch and tracked flight carry on. If something should have ended while the
  phone was off, it ends as soon as the phone is back on.
- Homework reminders are set again. Homework you already ticked off stays quiet.
- If the secure screenshot gesture stops working after an update, switch the
  Dynamic Island off and on again in Accessibility.

**No internet**
- Lookups (weather, definitions, METAR, "how far is …") say "You're offline"
  instead of a confusing error. An update download gives up on a dead
  connection and says why.

**You said no to a permission**
- Kinetic says what it needed. If Android has stopped asking (after you said no
  twice), Kinetic opens its own page in Samsung Settings. Allow it under
  **Permissions** there.
- Commands like "text mom …" still work without the permission: Messages opens
  with the text typed in.

**Storage is full**
- The Vault camera, Vault video, Move to Vault, secure screenshots and updates
  tell you when storage is full, instead of failing silently.

**Other**
- The island looks empty, or got stuck: pull it down and push it back up. If it
  stays wrong, switch the Dynamic Island off and on in Settings ▸ Setup.
- Very large photos open at the size they're shown, so they don't crash
  Kinetic. If a photo is too big to open, the Vault shows nothing instead of
  crashing.
- Anything else: `report problem` and send the file.

## 15. Things Android or Samsung don't allow

- Apps can't shrink an app back into its icon when it closes.
- Apps can't tell *which* fingerprint was used, when a face scan starts, or
  which unlock method worked.
- The flashlight's beam *width* can't be changed on Android phones.
- Samsung's notification shade can't be detected, and Kinetic can't hide
  Samsung's Do Not Disturb icon (QuickStar can't either).
- Launchers can't block an app opened from Recents or another app's link.
- One UI sometimes keeps the lock-screen wallpaper for Samsung's own wallpapers.
- App shortcuts in the long-press menu only appear while Kinetic is the default
  Home app.

## 16. Installing from a computer

You only need this if you build Kinetic yourself instead of using Updates.

1. In Android Studio, open the **kinetic-launcher** folder (the one containing
   `settings.gradle.kts`).
2. Turn on Developer options: Samsung Settings ▸ About phone ▸ Software
   information ▸ tap **Build number** seven times.
3. **USB:** Developer options ▸ **USB debugging**, connect the cable and allow
   the computer. **Wi-Fi:** Developer options ▸ **Wireless debugging** ▸ *Pair
   device with pairing code*, then pair from the computer.
4. Pick the phone in Android Studio and press **Run**. For daily use choose the
   **release** build variant.
